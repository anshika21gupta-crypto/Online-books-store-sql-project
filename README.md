# Online-books-store-sql-project
Excited to share that I have just completed a hands-on data analysis project: Online Bookstore Database Analysis using SQL! 📚📊  To simulate real-world e-commerce data operations, I designed and queried a relational database schema across three primary datasets: Books, Customers, and Orders. 
Key Business Questions Explored
Sales & Revenue Performance: Calculated total revenue, evaluated order distributions across genres, and identified top-selling authors.

Customer Segmentation: Analyzed multi-order customers, high-value purchasers (> $30 per order), and geographic distribution of top buyers.

Inventory & Stock Reconciliation: Identified low-stock items, tracked genre pricing averages, and calculated remaining stock after fulfilling order quantities using null-safe joins.

🛠️ Technical Concepts & SQL Toolkit
Multi-Table Joins (INNER JOIN, LEFT JOIN): Relational data mapping between customers, orders, and product catalogs.

Aggregations & Filtering: SUM(), AVG(), COUNT(), GROUP BY, and HAVING to isolate trends and high-frequency purchase behaviors.

Conditional Logic & Data Handling: Utilized COALESCE() and arithmetic operations for accurate stock fulfillment calculations.

Data Ordering & Windowing: Leveraged ORDER BY, DISTINCT, and LIMIT to identify top performers and outliers.

Continuous practice with real-world schemas helps bridge the gap between querying data and driving business decisions. Feedback and suggestions are always welcome!
